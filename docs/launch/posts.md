## Show HN
**Title:** Show HN: Lyra, word-by-word synced lyrics over the music video (Windows and Android, MIT)

I built Lyra because the lyrics view in my music app felt like an afterthought. It reads what you play (Spotify desktop on Windows, any media app on Android), fetches synced lyrics from LRCLIB, and fills in each word as it is sung over the music video. There is a floating widget, a fullscreen view, an Android screen-saver mode with a clock, and rooms: share a link and friends see your lyrics live in a browser (WebRTC, no server).

No account, no ads, no analytics. Electron + React + TypeScript, Kotlin shell for Android, shared core.
Repo: https://github.com/DhruvHarani1/lyra · Demo: https://lyra-website-rho.vercel.app

Happy to hear what is missing, especially for non-Latin scripts.
